How to Join

Membership in the Sons of the American Revolution is open to any man 18 or older who can document lineal descent from a patriot who supported American independence during the Revolutionary War.

Am I Eligible?

You may qualify if you can trace your family line back to an ancestor who, between 1774 and 1783, provided military, civil, or patriotic service to the cause of American independence. This includes soldiers and militia, but also signers of oaths of allegiance, suppliers of materials, and other recognized forms of patriotic service.

Don't already know your lineage? That's completely normal — many members start the process not yet knowing their qualifying patriot. Our chapter and the state Registrar can help you research it.

Steps to Join

  1. Reach out. Contact us using the form below or visit a chapter meeting as a guest — no commitment required.
  2. Connect with a mentor. The Alabama Society Registrar pairs prospective members with a compatriot who helps research and document your patriot ancestor.
  3. Complete your application. Your mentor and the Registrar's office will guide you through the required lineage documentation.
  4. Attend a meeting. New members are welcomed at an upcoming chapter meeting — see our Meeting Info page for schedule and location.

Tell Us You're Interested

Fill out the short form below and we'll follow up by email — typically within a few days. Your information is also shared with the Alabama Society State Registrar to help connect you with a mentor.

State Registrar Contact

If you'd prefer to reach out directly, the Alabama Society State Registrar, Ron Bearden, assists prospective members across all Alabama chapters with lineage research and mentor pairing. Contact details are available via alssar.org.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already know my patriot ancestor?

No. Many prospective members start the process without knowing their qualifying ancestor. Your mentor and the Registrar's office can help with the research.

Is there a cost to join?

Yes, there are national, state, and chapter dues, along with a one-time application fee. Current amounts are listed in the Fees & Dues chart on alssar.org, or your mentor can walk you through the current figures.

Can I visit a meeting before applying?

Absolutely — guests are always welcome. See our Meeting Info page for the next date and location.

How long does the application process take?

It varies based on how quickly lineage documentation can be gathered, but most applications are processed within a few months once the paperwork is submitted.

Do I have to live in the Shoals area to join this chapter?

Chapter membership is generally based on where you reside. If you live outside our area, we're happy to point you to the appropriate chapter, or discuss options with you directly.
