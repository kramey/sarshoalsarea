Alabama Society, Sons of the American Revolution — honoring the patriots of the Shoals area and preserving their legacy through service, education, and remembrance.

Interested in Joining?

Who We Are

The Shoals Area Chapter brings together descendants of American Revolutionary War patriots for fellowship, historical preservation, and community service across the Shoals region of Alabama. We are a small, active chapter and welcome visitors to our meetings.

Think You Might Qualify?

If you can trace your lineage to a patriot who supported American independence, you may be eligible to join. Visit our How to Join page for eligibility criteria and next steps, or reach out any time — we're happy to help you get started.
