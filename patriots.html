Our Patriot Ancestors

Every member of our chapter descends from a Patriot who helped win American independence. These are their stories.

Membership in the Sons of the American Revolution isn't honorary — it's earned through documented proof of lineage back to a man or woman who supported the cause of American independence between 1774 and 1783, whether as a soldier, sailor, civil officer, or civilian patriot. Behind every member of the Shoals Area Chapter stands one of these Patriots, researched and verified through the SAR's genealogical process.

Below are the Patriot ancestors represented in our chapter today. As new members join, we'll add their Patriots to this page.
